# PulsarBox

Pulsar synthesis (Curtis Roads) for OceanAudio: the node is
`src/nodes/scPulsarBox.h`, the synths are `pulsarbox.scd` here.

Every voice emits pulses at its Fundamental: below about 20 Hz you hear a
rhythm, above it a pitch. Each pulse holds 1 to 4 formant layers. A layer is a
pulsaret table read at the layer's formant frequency (in Hz, or as a ratio of
the fundamental), under an envelope table lasting `Cycles` periods of the
formant. The formant sets where the spectrum peaks, the fundamental sets the
harmonics under that peak, and Cycles sets the peak's width.

Masking removes pulses: Probability, Burst / Rest, and a Sieve of up to 32
steps. Each pulse can land on a different output (Voice, Cycle, Random, Layer
modes), with per-pulse jitter of rate, formant, level and output.

- SynthDefs: `PulsarBox_<voices>_<outputs>`, voices 1..8, outputs 1..16
  (128 defs, stock `GrainBuf`, no plugin). Evaluate `pulsarbox.scd` and copy
  `CompiledSynthdefs/PulsarBox/` to the app's
  `data/Supercollider/Synthdefs/Defaults/PulsarBox/`.
- Tables: built-in pulsarets and envelopes are generated by the node. Any mono
  or multichannel WAV works (first channel, resampled to 2048 points, peak
  normalised). The browser lists `data/Supercollider/PulsarBox/nuPG_TABLES`
  (Marcin Pietruszewski's nuPG 26, commit
  `a97ae4eb93b86ab7edec69b3eb469318b58dd183`, under its `nuPG_LICENSE`) and
  `data/Supercollider/PulsarBox/Tables` (your own).
- Presets: `Presets/` here (made by `tools/pulsarbox_presets.py`), deployed
  to `data/Supercollider/PulsarBox/Presets`.
